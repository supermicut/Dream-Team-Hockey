The loop

Something to chase every day.

One match is one ticket. Short sessions, and progress that builds over seasons.

01

Set the lineup

Four forward lines, three pairs, two goalies. Off-position players lose rating.

02

Play the match

Spend a ticket. Tickets refill one every 10 minutes, up to 10.

03

Earn rewards

Hockey Bucks and Training Whistles for every result, more for a win.

04

Scout and train

Open packs, level players to 50, release the ones you don't need.

05

Climb the league

38 games, top four promoted. Four divisions to the top.

4 · 3 · 2

Lines and Chemistry

Fill a line from one nation and every player on it gets +2 to every stat.
